# Farm System V1 — Business Rules and Invariants

## Enforcement principle

Every invariant below must be enforced through Cloud Functions as the trusted write layer. The required flow is `UI → Cloud Functions → business validation → Firestore transaction → Firestore`. UI validation is helpful but is not authoritative, and client-side validation is never a security boundary. Firestore Rules alone may be insufficient for cross-document invariants and concurrency control. Cloud Functions implementation is intentionally out of scope for this documentation stage. Historical records are append-only or archived; no user flow may hard-delete transaction history.

## Breeding and identity invariants

1. **One cage : one pair for the same time interval.** A `cageAssignments` interval `[startsOn, endsOn)` may not overlap another interval for the same `cageId`. An absent `endsOn` means open-ended.
2. **One pair : one cage for the same time interval.** A `cageAssignments` interval may not overlap another interval for the same `pairId`. This creates a one-to-one temporal relation; changing a cage closes the old assignment and creates a new assignment.
3. **Pair membership is canonical.** A valid active pair has exactly two active `pairMembers`: one `role=male`, one `role=female`. The birds must be distinct and each bird must have compatible current `sexHistory` evidence. One bird cannot be an active member of two pairs for overlapping intervals.
4. **Cycles belong to the pair at creation.** A breeding cycle stores immutable `pairId` and `startedOn`. Closing/re-pairing later must not rewrite prior cycles.
5. **Egg identity is immutable.** `cycleId`, `sequenceNo`, and `laidOn` cannot change after creation. `sequenceNo` must be unique within a cycle and positive.
6. **One egg : zero or one bird.** A farm-hatched bird must have one immutable `eggId`; there must be at most one `birds` document with any given `eggId`. The egg is marked `hatched` only when that relation is valid. There is no `chicks` canonical entity.
7. **Parentage is derived.** No write path accepts `fatherId` or `motherId` as canonical fields on `birds`. For a bird with `eggId`, derive parents from `EGG → BREEDING_CYCLE → PAIR → PAIR_MEMBER`. Parentage displayed on a completed handover may be a snapshot for historical accuracy, but it must not feed back into the breeding chain.
8. **Ring IDs are globally unique.** `birds.ringId` is required, trimmed and converted to the locale-independent uppercase canonical form before storage/comparison, immutable, and unique across all bird records, including archived/deceased/sold birds. Normalization must not alter internal characters: `GC-001 = gc-001`, `GC-001 ≠ GC 001`, and `GC-001 ≠ GC001`.
9. **Sex is evidence-based.** The current sex is derived from the latest valid `sexHistory` entry. A pair may not be activated if current evidence is unknown or incompatible with its assigned member role.

## Kinship / Inbreeding Policy — V1

10. **Canonical pedigree only.** Before activating a pair, the system must be able to inspect known pedigree through `BIRD → EGG → BREEDING_CYCLE → PAIR → PAIR_MEMBER → BIRD`. User-entered `fatherId`/`motherId` fields are prohibited as a parentage source of truth.
11. **Policy C activation gate.** `parent × offspring` and `sibling × sibling` relationships are blocked and the pair must not activate. Other detectable kinship relationships produce a warning only. When pedigree information is insufficient, the result is `unknown`; the system must not infer a relationship.
12. **No numerical coefficient.** V1 does not define or require a genetic coefficient or numerical inbreeding algorithm.

## Commercial invariants

10. **Reservation is independent from sale.** A reservation reserves a bird for a customer but does not itself sell the bird. At most one active reservation may exist for one bird. Converting to a sale records a reference to the reservation without deleting it.
11. **Payment is an independent receipt.** A payment belongs to exactly one reservation or one sale, never both. A deposit is a payment attached to a reservation with `purpose=deposit`. A payment is retained when its reservation becomes cancelled, expired, or completed.
12. **Refund limit and operator decision.** For each payment, total refund amounts cannot exceed `payment.amount`. A refund always references its payment. When a reservation is cancelled or expired, the operator explicitly selects full refund, partial refund, or no refund; partial refund requires the actual amount. Every decision records its reason and transaction date. There is no automatic refund policy based on cancellation reason.
13. **One completed sale per bird.** At most one `sales` record with `status=completed` may reference a given `birdId`. The check must run atomically with the status transition. A draft, confirmed, or cancelled sale does not satisfy this invariant.
14. **Completed commercial records are immutable in identity.** Completed sales, refunds, giveaways, payments, deliveries, handovers, and their timeline events cannot have their identity references or financial amount removed/reassigned. Corrections use compensating records and timeline events.
15. **Completed sale and completed giveaway are exclusive.** A bird cannot have both; open Sale commitments (`draft|confirmed`) and open Giveaway commitments (`planned`) also block conflicting progress through trusted transactions. Giveaway completion is agreement completion only and must not change the Bird to `given_away`. Physical custody changes only when one completed `sourceType=giveaway` Handover for that completed Giveaway records its immutable recipient snapshot; that transaction changes the Bird to `given_away`. Sale keeps the corresponding `completed Sale → completed Handover → sold` lifecycle.
16. **Shipping is historical per delivery.** Each delivery stores immutable `distanceKm`, `freeDistanceKm`, `pricePerKm`, and trusted-system-derived `shippingFee`. At creation, the trusted write calculates `shippingFee = max(distanceKm - freeDistanceKm, 0) * pricePerKm`; a client-supplied fee is never authoritative. Do not later recompute completed delivery fees using a global configuration, including after a global shipping price changes.
17. **Sale timeline is append-only.** Each material commercial state change creates a `saleTimeline` event. The timeline explains history but does not replace `sales.status` as source of truth. Reservation-only events do not create `saleTimeline` records because `saleId` is required. A reservation and its deposit/refund history remain represented by their canonical records until a sale is created; no synthetic backfill is permitted.
18. **Price History is informational and append-only.** A `priceHistory` entry is immutable historical evidence only. Entries may overlap and no trusted write may automatically select, inherit, synchronize, or propagate a Price History amount into a Reservation or Sale.
19. **Agreement-price snapshots are paired and immutable.** `reservations` and `sales` either omit both `agreedPrice` and `currency`, or store a finite decimal `agreedPrice > 0` with `currency=THB`. Missing price is not zero or free. A direct Sale accepts only its explicit operator snapshot; a Reservation-to-Sale transaction copies the Reservation pair exactly and rejects caller repricing. Existing historical records are not backfilled.
20. **Sale Timeline operator read is bounded.** Timeline visibility is operator-only, Sale-detail-only, chronological, and read-only. It does not expose public/commercial data through Passport and cannot derive or override `sales.status`.

## Lifecycle and retention invariants

21. **No hard deletion of historical records.** Sales, reservations, payments, refunds, giveaways, deliveries, handovers, price history, weight history, sex history, documents, photos, pair memberships, cage assignments, cycles, and eggs are retained. Use `status`, `archivedAt`, `supersededBy`, or correction links instead.
19. **Handover recipient snapshot is immutable and minimal.** It contains exactly required non-empty `name` and optional `phone` and `address`; later Customer changes never alter the completed handover snapshot.
19. **No destructive re-parenting.** A farm-hatched bird's `eggId` is immutable. If a data correction is needed, preserve the original record, record the reason and actor, and use an explicit governed correction process.
20. **Temporal data is interval-based.** End dates must be on/after start dates. Overlap checks treat absent end dates as infinity and must be evaluated atomically.
21. **Passport is a view.** Public Passport access resolves a unique public token to canonical data only when `passportStatus=published`; `draft` and `disabled` return safe not-found. Tokens are trusted-server generated, at least 128-bit cryptographically random, unique, non-sequential, and non-predictable; rotation invalidates the old token. Its public V1 fields are bird photo, ring ID, mutation, birth/hatch date, available sex, origin, safe derived parentage, handover date, and passport status. Only active BIRD-owned photos/documents with `isPublicOnPassport=true` may contribute their explicit public allow-listed metadata. Customer contact/address, payment information, internal notes, internal operational data, storage/internal metadata, and unauthorized price/transaction data are private by default and must never be exposed through `publicToken`. A managed public Photo uses a short-lived opaque BMB media handle and is streamed by a trusted endpoint that re-checks Passport and Photo eligibility for every request. Private Storage bucket/object paths, canonical IDs, Passport tokens, and direct GCS URLs are never returned. Media responses use `Cache-Control: no-store`; archive, draft/disabled status, and token rotation revoke subsequent media requests. Public Documents remain metadata-only.
22. **Public Passport has one token-only route.** V1 public access uses `/passport/<publicToken>` without authentication and resolves exclusively through `getBirdPassport`. The route never contains a Bird or other internal ID. Admin Open/Copy actions and the locally generated QR encode this same URL. Draft, disabled, invalid, and rotated tokens use the same neutral unavailable response; republishing re-enables the current token and does not generate another token when one already exists.
23. **Bird asset intake is verified.** New Bird-owned Photos and Documents are finalized only after an operator-authorized, server-controlled Storage intake verifies an exact Bird-scoped object path, actual content type, and actual size. Metadata alone is never proof that an object exists, and Storage path alone never establishes Bird ownership. Photos allow JPEG, PNG, WebP up to 10 MB. Documents allow PDF, JPEG, PNG up to 15 MB. This validates Storage metadata only; it is not malware scanning.
24. **Asset terminal lifecycle.** Active Photos may be archived. Active Documents may be archived or superseded only with an active, same-Bird replacement Document; the old Document retains immutable `supersededByDocumentId`. Archived and superseded objects/metadata are retained and never publicly eligible. `isPublicOnPassport` records publication intent and may remain true historically, but public eligibility additionally requires active status.
25. **Asset intake support state is private.** `assetIntakes` is server-only implementation-support state, not one of the 21 canonical business collections, not directly client-readable, and never Passport data.
26. **Delivery is sale-only in V1.** A Delivery belongs to one completed Sale. Giveaway- and Handover-owned Delivery workflows are outside V1 and are not accepted by trusted writes.

## Required transactional checks

The minimum Cloud Functions trusted-write operations are `activatePair`, `assignPairToCage`, `createBirdFromEgg`, `checkRingIdAvailability`, `completeSale`, `refundPayment`, and `completeHandover`. `checkRingIdAvailability` is advisory only and never creates a durable claim; durable ring uniqueness is enforced atomically when a Bird is created or updated. Each authoritative write performs business validation and a Firestore transaction. Creating/closing cage assignments, creating a breeding cycle, completing a giveaway, and any other write that can violate a cross-document invariant must also use this trusted layer. Firestore client-only checks are insufficient because two concurrent clients can otherwise violate these invariants.
